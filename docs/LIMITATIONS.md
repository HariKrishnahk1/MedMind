# Data & Model Limitations

## 1. Synthetic Data Origin
- The dataset is synthetically generated using defined Gaussian, Poisson, and Markovian statistical distributions. It does not represent real patient medical records from an IRB-approved clinical registry.

## 2. Clinical Threshold Validation
- Decision threshold optimization ($th=0.50$) is based on mathematical empirical optimization (F1-score / False Alert Rate minimization) and has NOT been validated in a prospective real-world clinical trial.

## 3. Generalizability Limitations
- Performance metrics reflect synthetic cohort characteristics and may not generalize to specialized patient populations (e.g. pediatric ICU, neonatal, or trauma units).
